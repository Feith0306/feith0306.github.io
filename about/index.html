About Me

I am a master’s student (2024–2027) in Control Engineering at the School of Automation and Electrical Engineering (SAEE), University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB), supervised by Prof. Yongliang Yang. I also work closely with Dr. Jaeyoung Lee on reinforcement learning and control. Prior to that, I obtained my B. Eng degree in Automation at USTB in 2024.

Currently, my research focuses on the theoretical foundations of intelligent control and reinforcement learning, while exploring their potential applications to embodied AI and autonomous systems, including robots, UAVs, and CAVs.

For more details, please see my CV (English) or CV (中文). (Last update: November 20, 2026)
